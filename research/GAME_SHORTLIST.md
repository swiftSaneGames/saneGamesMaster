# Original concept shortlist

These are **new design proposals**, not released games, validated market winners or trademark-cleared names. References describe what to research, not permission to copy expression or assets. Each reference title links to its publisher source through `games_100.csv` and `studios_20.csv`.

**Research first:** concepts 1–3 directly match the owner's pizza, petrol-station and pet-care examples. **Research next:** 4–8 reuse management foundations. **Explore later:** 9–12 introduce larger systems or a separate decision framework. These groups are prototype suitability judgments, not download-based P1/P2 ranks.

| # | Working concept | Reference research | Original differentiator to prototype | Content and testing concern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neighborhood Pizza Routes | Pizza Ready; Idle Restaurant Tycoon | Balance a walk-in kitchen with short neighborhood delivery routes and customer preferences. | Distinct locations, recipe/staff unlocks; validate queue congestion and save migration. |
| 2 | Roadside Rest Stop | Gas Station Inc.; Vehicle Masters | Combine fuel service, a small cafe and a repair bay; different traffic patterns change staffing choices. | Distinct road stops, vehicle types and service upgrades; test pathfinding and resource deadlocks. |
| 3 | Pet Haven Network | Paw Care; Pet Rescue Empire Tycoon | Match pets to enrichment activities and adoptive homes instead of simply maximizing throughput. | New facilities and pet routines; test gentle content, readable needs and non-exploitative rewards. |
| 4 | Cozy Harbor Inns | My Perfect Hotel; Idle Inn Empire | Guests choose quiet, food or scenery; room layouts affect satisfaction and staff travel. | Distinct inns and staff skills; test navigation, tablet layout and economy balance. |
| 5 | Corner Market Cooperative | Mini Market; Idle Supermarket Tycoon | Customers request local bundles; supplier choices change freshness and waste. | Several neighborhoods and supply lines; test inventory conservation and offline state. |
| 6 | Coffee Stop Stories | Coffee Break; Perfect Coffee 3D | Remember regular customers while choosing between quick service and optional recipe-making tasks. | New stations, recipes and locations; test task interruptions and ad-return continuity. |

## Pilot method

Prototype one core loop at a time. Start with concept 1 unless stronger research supports another. Have an independent agent evaluate controls, originality, progression and device feasibility before expanding. A small prototype is a learning artifact, not a finished release counted toward 100.

For the chosen production concept, define distinct locations and progression arcs before estimating time. Maintain a content schema so new locations can reuse systems without becoming cosmetic duplicates. Keep the first three architecture experiments small enough to measure development, device testing and maintenance cost.
