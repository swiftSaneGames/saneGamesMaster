# Demand evidence and prioritization

## Separate three decisions

1. **Research order:** which reference best helps answer our current design question?
2. **Prototype order:** which original concept best fits our reusable foundations, resources and differentiation?
3. **Market priority:** which concept has supported current demand in its intended markets?

Do not label a prototype preference as a download ranking. The concept shortlist uses research/prototype groups only. All market priorities in this initial catalog remain `PENDING_DATA`.

## Required evidence for a demand claim

Record the exact app/store ID, platform, metric definition, start/end date, country scope, source, access date, observed value or interval, and whether it is measured or estimated. Preserve a private evidence snapshot when permitted. Mark estimation methodology and limitations. Do not treat a language parameter such as `hl=en_IN` as proof of Indian download counts.

A Google Play public install band is a lifetime lower-bound category, not an exact total or recent download figure. Compare only like-for-like measurements. A rank is ordinal, not a download count. Reviews and review growth are proxies, not sales. Publisher-wide cumulative downloads cannot be assigned to each title. Android observations do not establish iOS totals.

In this pack, the Pizza Ready listing displayed 10Cr+ (100 million+) and Idle Bank Tycoon displayed 1Cr+ (10 million+). These are retrieved listing observations, not independently audited measurements. Sources and limitations are recorded in `download_observations.csv`.

## No-cost research process

Start with official catalog pages and the real store listings. Then collect permitted, clearly dated chart observations and public publisher case studies. Historical case studies remain historical. Revisit the same source and scope rather than mixing unrelated charts. Add gameplay evidence only after actual play or review of an accessible recording.

Do not purchase analytics, bypass login/paywalls/CAPTCHAs, or invent unavailable monthly estimates. When current comparable data remains unavailable, leave demand unranked and select a small prototype using documented product fit. The owner explicitly permits quality to extend the timeline.

## Proposed market-priority rubric

Use this only after eligible evidence exists. Within the same platform, time window, geography and comparison set, normalize each measured criterion to 0–100. Suggested weighting: recent download volume 50%, growth 25%, repeated chart presence 15%, cross-platform corroboration 10%. These are proposed decision weights, not research findings. Do not compute a score when required fields are missing; leave it pending rather than renormalizing silently.

Separately document development effort, distinctiveness, age/privacy obligations, expected maintenance and ad tolerance. A large market signal can still produce a no-build decision. Revenue assumptions require fill rate, actual ad impressions and measured revenue per thousand impressions; raw installs alone do not establish profit.

## Evidence required before a game starts production

A distinct original concept; a short observed reference report; an independently tested playable loop; a cost/maintenance forecast; device performance feasibility; appropriate content/ad design; a source/asset license inventory; and an agent-reviewed scope. These are agent gates, not additional scheduled owner approvals. The owner's single release checkpoint remains mandatory.
